## 2026-11-21 (night): sunlight is no longer a free lunch (soil minerals, weak sun)
- Cap asked why anything eats meat when sunlight is free. Real answer (energy is not everything: minerals, weak sunlight, any energy store is exploitable) and the sim's flaw: photosynthesis was free and uncontested, so every dish became giant sun-blobs. Built: **soil minerals** (`w.nut`, one value per cell; photosynthesis needs and uses them; they return from eating waste, from every body that dies, a slow regrowth, and spread between cells; saved in the dish; new brain input `NUT`, NI=60; sun-eaters instinctively drift toward richer soil; plant pellets only grow in fertile ground), **weak sun** (`SUN.k` 0.052 to 0.045 after tuning), and a stronger plant supply (growFood rate 34 then 70). 👁 → **Soil richness** draws it.
- Tuning story (so nobody repeats it): with the old sun the soil limit barely mattered (everyone stayed 0.99 photosynthetic); with sun 0.02 photosynthesis died out entirely (every creature became a pellet-eater, 20-100 creatures); sun 0.045 with soil use 0.03 and plant growth 70 gives mixed outcomes by seed: some dishes stay sun-eaters and drain their soil, some turn to eating plants. Defaults are in `SUN`, `NUT` (sim.js) and `growFood`.
- Re-evolved: 12 runs of 3 years under the final rules (`scripts/evo11`), founders rebuilt (`founders.js`, 24 genomes), `BEST_SEED = 226` (5-6 species at every checkpoint over 3 hours), optional worlds A and B in `starter/` regenerated from seeds 409 and 402 and renamed.
- Honest numbers, no rescue anywhere: 0 of 64 fresh dishes went extinct in 1 hour and 0 of 24 in 3 hours; average population about 110 (the dish is less crowded than before). **Still no specialist hunters evolve** (diet stays near 0.02; creatures do bite each other a lot, 10-20k kills a year, mostly omnivore and cannibal biting because the meat gain floor is 0.15). Making true predators emerge probably needs a prey-density mechanism or a lower biting floor; not tried. The hand-wired hunter lineage still starts every new dish.

- **Meat-eaters now evolve (2026-11-22).** Cap said yes to making hunters evolve. Cause found: a mixed diet gave no payoff (plant digestion fell in a straight line as diet rose, and kills paid the same for any diet below 0.07). Changes in `sim.js`: plant digestion = `1 - diet^3 * 0.92` (a taste for meat is nearly free), kill gain floor 0.15 to 0.05 (generalists no longer free-ride), bite gain x1.3 to x4. Test overrides: globals `__PK`, `__MF`, `__BG` (env PK, MF, BG in `scripts/hunt-test.mjs`, `hunt-long.mjs`, `ext-test.mjs`). Result: 12 three-year runs (`scripts/evo12`) have average diets 0.12-0.28 (was 0.01-0.02), with real meat-leaning lineages (diet 0.3-0.56, up to 49 of 159 creatures in one dish). True carnivores above 0.6 still do not appear. The hand-wired hunter lineage was removed from Day 1 dishes (it starved within 40 seconds). Founders rebuilt from evo12 (24 genomes, 8 meat-eaters, one meat-eater lineage always among the five picked), `BEST_SEED = 62`, starter worlds regenerated from q506 and q508. No-rescue survival: 0/64 extinct at 1 h, 0/24 at 3 h (seeds 300-363 and 400-423). Two of twelve classic-start evolution runs (old hand-wired start) went extinct in the first 2 minutes; that is not the Day 1 start.
