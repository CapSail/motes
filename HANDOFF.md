## 2026-11-20 (later): evolving squiggle bodies
- Cap asked for procedurally generated squiggle shapes that evolve. Added 8 shape genes (segments, length, taper, curl, wiggle amplitude and frequency, limb count and length). Bodies are drawn as a curling, wiggling spine of segments with limbs and eyes. Shape also changes physics: long bodies run faster but turn slowly, limbs turn better but cost drag, strong wiggle swims cheaper. Shape genes count toward speciation, so new species look different. Old saves load with neutral padded shape genes (a New dish gives the varied founders). Headless run still stable; checked in the browser pane, no console errors. Pushed to main (live site updates in about a minute).

## 2026-11-20 (night): scents, toxin and temperature you can see; longer senses
- Cap could not tell that toxin/scent buttons did anything. Fixes: **overlays are always on** (all scents, heat, cold, toxin drawn together; the View button still isolates one); a hint line appears when you pick a tool; the brush glows in the tool's colour; motes in toxin get a purple ring; motes releasing a scent give off a coloured ripple.
- Motes now react. **3 new smell genes** (NB=36, `SC_A/B/C`): each mote is drawn to or repelled by each scent (old saves get random tastes). A brain-independent reflex steers by sniffing 26 and 52 units out; **everyone flees toxin** (less so with resistance); **everyone steers toward the temperature it prefers** and away from too hot/cold. `scripts/reflex-test.mjs` checks it: likers move toward a poured scent, haters away, toxin area ends up almost empty.
- **Sense range**: sight/hunting 30-140 became 50-240 (hunters +15%), and **smell of food reaches 1.5x** further than sight. Upkeep cost per range cut (0.003 to 0.0012). A first try at 60-320 with +30% hunters crashed the prey, so it was dialled back; seeds 1-2 stay healthy (pop 90-120).
- Scents now fade slower (decay 0.32 to 0.16/s) so they spread and linger.
- The starter dish was not re-evolved (brains still tuned for the short range); genes load padded. Not tested on a real phone.

## 2026-11-21: walls, short-term memory, new looks, QoL, and a smarter evolved dish
- **Walls are solid for the senses**: they block sight, smell (food and motes need a clear line) and the spread of scents/toxin; motes steer away from walls and turn when they bump one. `scripts/wall-test.mjs` checks it.
- **Short-term memory** (NI=59): each mote remembers where it last saw food and where it was last hurt (fades over about 15 s) plus a slow mood; founders use it. Selected mote shows a dotted line to the food it remembers.
- **Looks** (NB=41): six body styles (beads, smooth tube, diamonds, stars, rings, leaves), spikes, 0-4 eyes, dorsal frill, glow. Cosmetic with tiny energy costs. Old saves get random looks for the new genes.
- **Pods**: strongly bonded friends stay together and even out energy (gold line).
- **Disasters**: meteor tool and random meteors (only when pop > 90), ice age and drought buttons (Menu, Dish), drought also random when pop > 60. First version of these killed dishes (3 of 24 test dishes went extinct); softened, now 0 of 12 to 24 in 40 minutes.

- **Sense ranges** from the earlier round stay: sight 50-240, smell 1.5x.
- **Evolved dishes** (`scripts/evolve.mjs <seed> <years> <out> [maxPop] [resume.json]`, now can resume a dish): first batch of six 3-year runs lost nearly all hunters and parasitism exploded; fixed by making the temperature reflex only act when a mote is uncomfortable, steal rate 2.6 to 1.2 and efficiency 0.5 to 0.42, bite gain x1.3. Longer runs evolved clumping (swarm). Not tried on a real phone; frame rate in the browser pane was 89 fps with 470 motes on the default dish.
- Test scripts: `churn-test`, `events-test`, `wall-test`, `reflex-test`, `stab-test`, `spread-test`, `pick` (scores dishes, now rewards look variety). Debug flags for experiments: `NOMEM`, `NOREFLEX`, `BG` env vars in evolve.mjs.
